## NoMercy Torrent Downloader — v0.3.15

Downloads start.

### A magnet has no piece count, and a peer still sends a bitfield

A client that takes a magnet on knows the info hash and nothing else. It dials
with nought pieces, because the metadata saying how many there are is the very
thing it is dialling for — and nearly every client sends its bitfield the moment
the handshake is done.

A bitfield for nought pieces is nought bytes, so every one of those was read as a
protocol violation. The exception unwound out of the metadata fetch, the
conversation swallowed it as "one peer is one peer", and the peer was destroyed
on its first message.

Measured on a swarm with 1206 seeders: 175 peers dialled, nine handshakes
completed, and all nine gone before they could offer anything. That is why a
download sat at "fetching metadata" with no peer, no seed and no error until it
timed out, whether the plugin chose it or the owner pasted the magnet in by hand.

A bitfield is taken as it comes until there is a piece count to check it against,
and a `have` naming a piece past the end of one nobody could size is not a peer
misbehaving. What a peer holds cannot be acted on before the metadata anyway:
there are no pieces to ask it for.

The swarm test that had failed all evening now finds peers in three seconds, and
the file it names appears on disk while it runs.

### Everything else

Unchanged from 0.3.14, whose own fix — a client with no metadata no longer
announcing itself as a seed — was the half of this that made trackers answer with
peers at all.
