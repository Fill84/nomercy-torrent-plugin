## NoMercy Torrent Downloader — v0.8.4

Seeds and peers on the Downloads page are counted as what they are.

### What changed

- **A seed is counted as a seed.** A download that starts from a magnet meets its first peers before it
  knows how many pieces the torrent has. What such a peer said it has was never read against that count
  once it was known, so a seed met that early stood in the Peers column for as long as it stayed
  connected, and a peer that had said nothing stood in the Seeds column. A download arriving at seventy
  megabytes a second read "0 of 7" seeds beside "5 of 1" peers. It now reads what is there.
- **A peer met that early is asked for the pieces it finishes later.** A piece such a peer announced
  before the count was known was mostly thrown away, so the client never asked that peer for it.
- **The swarm is never shown smaller than what is connected.** The number after "of" is what the trackers
  last said, and a peer found through the DHT or a peer exchange is in no tracker's count. Five connected
  out of a tracker's one is now drawn "5 of 5".

### Not in this version

- Anything else. The searching, the sources, the pages and the plugin's own browser are those of 0.8.3.

### Upgrading

Install through the plugin catalogue, then restart the server once. Nothing needs approving again: the
manifest asks for nothing 0.8.3 did not. Settings, show switches and downloads carry over.
