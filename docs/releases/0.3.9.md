## NoMercy Torrent Downloader — v0.3.9

Keeps a TV or anime library complete by downloading missing episodes over BitTorrent and handing each
one to the server's own encoder.

**This build delivers episodes.** On 25 August 2026 it took Sugar S02E04 from missing to encoded into
the library with nothing done by hand — found, downloaded, staged, dispatched with its own episode
id, and the copies cleaned up after the encode landed. That is the whole chain, watched rather than
trusted.

### What it does

- Derives what is missing from the library itself, every cycle. Nothing is stored as fact.
- Searches the name sources rather than an indexer's rendering of a title, and judges every release
  against the profile: resolution, codec, language, seeders, season packs, blacklists.
- Refuses what it should and says why on the Skipped page — h265, 2160p and foreign audio among them,
  and a 1.2 GB executable named after an episode, refused before a byte of it was fetched.
- Downloads with its own BitTorrent client: DHT, PEX, magnet metadata, resume, port mapping, and no
  upload at all on a public swarm.
- Stages the finished video under the episode's own name and asks the encoder for it, retrying a
  refusal rather than forgetting it.
- Anime is searched by both season and absolute numbering, derived from the library.

### What it does not do yet

- **It still reaches into the server by name.** Five server types, in one file, resolved through
  `IServiceProvider`. That goes when the plugin contract can express an encode — media-server #30 and
  #35 — and it is the whole of what 0.4.0 is.
- **A show you have just added is invisible to it.** A show counts as yours once at least one episode
  is on disk, because nothing else tells a show you added from a row identification imported on a
  guess. Widening that rule was tried and put the plugin on 479 grabs in an afternoon, 456 of them
  one show nobody had asked for. It is fixed properly by media-server #36 and #34.
- **An episode already on disk at a lower quality is not re-downloaded.** Deliberately out of scope.
- Films are out of scope entirely.

### What changed since the chain closed

All eleven findings of a full audit of the source, and **not one of them changed what the plugin
does**. Each removed work, moved a rule to one place, or put a seam where the next change already
lands: the rule for whose show it is is written once instead of twice; a tick asks the library each
question once instead of eight times; the database prepares its file once a run instead of some
21,600 times a day; every piece of periodic housekeeping is in the cadence named for it; and the
encode sits behind a port, so the day the contract lands is a new class beside the old one rather
than surgery.

### Install

1. Extract `NoMercy.Plugin.TorrentDownloader-0.3.9.zip` into `<server>/plugins/`.
2. Restart the server.
3. Enable the plugin and grant consent when prompted.
4. Open its settings and set the **incomplete** and **intake** folders. Nothing downloads until both
   are set.

It registers four scheduled jobs — `transfers`, `feed`, `search` and `maintenance` — each visible
separately in the server's job list. Cadences are registered once at server start, so a changed cron
applies at the next restart.

The package carries every assembly the plugin needs and SQLite for all twenty-one platforms; a plugin
folder missing its dependencies loads as nothing at all, with nothing to say why.

### Requires

NoMercy MediaServer with the plugin ABI `10.0` contract. Built against
`NoMercy.Plugins.Abstractions` **0.1.478**, packed from the released server, not from `dev` — `dev`
carries a fixed `0.1.404` that never moves.
