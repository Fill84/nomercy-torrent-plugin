## NoMercy Torrent Downloader — v0.6.0

The version where you decide what is downloaded, show by show, and a release name decides the rest.

**The show list:** every show and anime is switched on or off on its own, with settings of its own or its library's. One quality profile for everything is gone.

**The search:** release names come from the name sources' feeds on every run, are judged against the show's settings before any indexer is asked, and the torrent the most indexers list is the one downloaded.

### The show list

The plugin opens on an overview of your libraries: the run's status line with Run and Stop, then one block per tv and anime library.

- **A block holds the library's preferences** on one line with an Edit button, and a table of its shows: show, year, missing, quality, codec, tags and state.
- **Every row has its own switch and its own Settings button.** A row is On or Off. No switch turns a whole library on or off.
- **Only your shows are listed:** a show the library holds a video file of, or one you switched on. The server keeps a row for every show it ever identified, and on a real library twelve of sixty-nine were shows nobody had added, which read as recommendations.
- **The missing column counts every listed show's aired episodes with no video file**, switched on or off, season 0 only where that show takes specials. It is counted from the episodes themselves: the server's own episode count read nought for 69 shows while 57 of them held files.
- **Switched-on shows come first**, each group in alphabetical order, 50 rows a page with Previous and Next.
- **A show switched on is searched straight away with its library's settings.** You open its form only to give it settings of its own. Seven shows switched on from their rows used to wait for a Save nobody had reason to press.
- **A show switched off has nothing searched and nothing downloaded**, and keeps the settings set on it. A download of such a show is cancelled and its files deleted, unless its episode is already staged for the encoder.

### Settings per show and per library

- **Quality** is one of 2160p, 1080p, 720p and 480p. **Codec** is any, h264, h265, xvid or divx.
- **Specials** decide whether season 0 is searched for.
- **English only** refuses a name claiming any other language and takes a name claiming none. A plain `DUAL` counts as dual audio.
- **Wishes, musts and forbidden** are lists of tags, typed as comma-separated text or one tag at a time, and each field shows an example. A tag is any word a release name carries: a language, a source, a group.
- **A show follows its library** for quality, codec, specials and English only until it sets its own. Its tag lists are the library's and its own together; a tag the two put in different lists counts only where the show put it.
- **A library's codec is any, and its specials and English only are off**, until you choose otherwise.
- **A show with no quality on itself or its library has nothing searched.**
- Below a show's form is what applies to it, with the library counted in.

### The Settings page

- **No quality, codec, tag or language setting.** They belong to a show and a library now, and a save that still names one is refused by name.
- **A run can start every 15 minutes, 30 minutes, hour, 6 hours, 12 hours or once a day.** Hourly stays the default. An expression under Show advanced that fires twice within 15 minutes is refused.

### Release names, read every run

- **The feeds of PreDB, srrDB, PreDB.net and SceneSource are read at once** at the start of every run. A name is taken for an episode when it names that show, season and episode.
- **An episode no feed named is looked up** in every name source's search as `Show SxxEyy`, with no quality and no year.
- **A name names exactly one episode.** A season pack, a run of episodes or an anime post under its absolute number names none, and is never searched for.
- **Nyaa is an indexer, for anime too.** The four name sources are the same for a show and an anime.
- **A name is judged against its show's settings before any indexer is asked.** Its resolution is the show's quality, its codec the show's codec, it carries every must and no forbidden tag. With a codec chosen, a name that does not say its codec is refused. A tag is a whole word of the name: `WEB` is in `ATVP.WEB-DL` and not in `WEBRip`.
- **No name that passes, no search.** The plugin builds no search term of its own.
- **The name pool is gone.** Nothing is kept between runs.

### One round of indexers, and one winner

- **Names are asked in wish groups**, the names carrying the most wishes first. A group that finds nothing hands over to the group with one wish fewer, down to names with none. A wish no name carries leaves the show downloadable.
- **The first-choice indexers are asked one after another**: TorrentBay then LimeTorrents for a show, Nyaa then TorrentBay then LimeTorrents for an anime. Every other enabled indexer is then asked at the same time. A find on a first-choice indexer does not end the round.
- **Each name is asked exactly**, and as words only where the exact name found nothing. A question already asked of an indexer is not asked again in that run.
- **A row counts only when its title is the name**, with case, punctuation and the site's own tag set aside. A row is not judged against the show's settings.
- **Most indexers print no hash**, so a row that is the name has its own page read for one.
- **Rows with the same info hash are one torrent**, carrying every tracker any of them gave.
- **The torrent on the most indexers wins.** Level, the one found first wins, which is a first-choice indexer's find, and Nyaa's for an anime. A release or hash still refused takes no part.
- **Only the winner is offered to the client.** The next one is offered only when the winner's magnet or torrent cannot be read from any indexer that listed it.
- **An episode with no torrent says why** on the Activity page, and is searched for again next run.

### What went

- **The global quality profile.** A configuration saved with it still loads, and the next save leaves it out.
- **The search ladder.** No site is asked the episode or the season with or without a resolution any more.
- **Ranking on seeders and a site's priority.** Seeders and a site's rating decide nothing.
- **The merge by name**, and a season settled by a pack.
- **The two-hash race.** One release under two hashes no longer starts both. A download an earlier version put in a folder of its own is still staged from it.

### A site that fails sits out the run

- **A challenge still there after a solve is solved again** and the question asked again, twice at most.
- **A site that does not answer is asked a second time.**
- **Still failing, it is left out of the rest of the run**, and the Sources page says why. A name source whose feed or search fails gives no names for the rest of the run, and the run carries on.

### TorrentBay had named no torrent since 30 August

TorrentBay names a torrent only through a signed request. The plugin sent it from a browser tab opened fresh for it, on no page of the site, ever since tabs began opening and closing per task, so it failed for every row. It now goes over plain HTTP in the session and with the clearance the listing was read with, and the browser no longer posts at all.

### The pages

- **The overview is the landing page.** What the dashboard showed during a run is the **Activity** page. The Shows page is gone.
- **The Queue lists the episodes being searched for**, from the shows switched on with a quality, and every row has a **Search now** button.
- **No Skipped page, and nothing about a refused name is stored.** A real server's history held 5,851 refusals against 219 lines of everything else, every one a name the settings refused before an indexer was asked. A refusal is said on the Activity page while the run is going, with the name and the reason, and goes when that episode is decided. Allow-anyway went with it.
- **History keeps what became of a grab**: decided, grabbed, dispatched or failed.

### Upgrading

The plugin database migrates itself on the first start, from 012 to 016:
- settings per show and per library are added;
- the name pool's table goes;
- English only is added to both;
- the refusals an older version wrote are deleted.

**Every show starts switched off, so nothing is searched until you switch shows on.** Downloads already running for a show that is not switched on, or has no quality anywhere, are cancelled and their files deleted, unless the episode is staged.

**Set each library's quality once**, with Edit on its block. The old profile's quality is not carried over, and a library with no quality has nothing searched for the shows that follow it.

### Not in this release

- **A library's quality is not read from its encoding profile.** The server does not yet tell a plugin which encoding presets a library uses or what resolution they reach (media-server #56). Until it does, you set a library's quality.
